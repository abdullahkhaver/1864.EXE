Learn it, build it, share it.

We're run by GCU students, for GCU students and the wider student community in Lahore. Through the year we organise events where people get hands-on with technology, meet others who care about it, and leave knowing a bit more than when they came in.

  • Competitions

    Hackathons and challenges where teams solve real problems against the clock, like 1864.EXE.

  • Workshops and talks

    Practical sessions on the tools and skills students actually use, led by people who use them.

  • Community

    A place to find teammates, ask questions and work on projects with other students.
